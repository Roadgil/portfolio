APAC · GLOBAL PLANNING
Hot Part · 긴급 할당
Hot Parts · Urgent Allocation
재고가 모자란 부품은 주간 소모량과 입고 예정분으로 몇 주를 버티는지 계산해서, 추가 수량과 도착 기한을 붙여 APAC 플래너에게 올린다. 요청은 글로벌 할당 회의 안건으로 이어진다.
For short parts, works out how many weeks the incoming quantity covers at the weekly burn rate, then raises the extra quantity and arrival date to the APAC planner. The request goes onto the global allocation meeting agenda.
소모성 hot part: 주 30~40개 소모, 입고 30개는 1주치 → 다음 주까지 30~40개 추가 요청Consumable hot part: 30 to 40 used a week, the 30 arriving cover one week, so 30 to 40 more requested for the following week
오픈 오더가 없어 할당이 막힌 건은 서비스팀 오더를 받아 약 1시간 만에 번호 회신, Oracle과 BI 반영 시차는 화면 캡처로 설명Where allocation was blocked by a missing open order, service orders were raised and returned within about an hour; the Oracle to BI reporting lag explained with a screenshot
장비가 멈춘 고객 건은 Customer Down 작업지시를 먼저 만들어 할당 근거로 제공For customers with a system down, Customer Down work orders created first to back the allocation

Options Before New Supply

Before asking for new stock, compares engineer trunk stock, in-country rebalance and breaking down upgrade kits, then replies with the option that loses the least inventory and why.
업그레이드 키트를 뜯으면 나머지 구성품을 팔 수 없게 된다는 점을 설명, 엔지니어와 협의해 차량 재고부터 소진하고 부족하면 재요청Explained that opening an upgrade kit leaves the other components unsellable; agreed with engineers to use trunk stock first and escalate again only if it runs out

Turns paperwork and trade-term problems on returns, rebalances and inbound shipments into English emails that leave the other side with a decision, not a puzzle.
미국 RMA 재고 88EA: Oracle 재고와 3PL 실사를 맞춘 뒤 미국 반송지 3곳에 매핑해 승인 요청 → 반송 62EA, 현지 폐기 26EA 확정 → 창고에 반송지별 포장 지시88 U.S. RMA units: Oracle stock reconciled with a 3PL count and mapped to three U.S. return destinations; approval gave 62 back to the U.S. and 26 for local scrap; the warehouse then packed by destination

Regulatory and Quality (RA/QA)

Licensing and quality calls belong to RA/QA; the job here is to find where shipping, clearance or stock is blocked, hand over the cause and the action needed, and confirm the release or disposition.
