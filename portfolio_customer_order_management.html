Detects service-team stock-request emails — DCD/consumables, defect/promotion, and chatbot orders — for Pick Release, and equipment-delivery-transfer emails for Ship Confirm. A third module codes all 14 steps of the cross-border inventory-rebalance export procedure, from the first alert through 3PL coordination, the Oracle transaction, the customs invoice, and the export declaration. Three modules sharing the same Oracle session.
